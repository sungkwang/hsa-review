Choose your HSA-Review data folder (once).

What to do first

The page reads and saves in the folder shown at the top (📁). If it is the wrong one, use Choose receipts folder; choose the folder that contains your year folders.

  1. Set Tax % Used only for items that show a price without tax. Orders with real tax data and whole-order totals don't use it.
  2. Choose a status for each item Pick Eligible, Doctor's note / LMN, or Not eligible. Likely items come first; unlikely ones are folded.
  3. Tick Reimbursed When you are paid back from the HSA, tick Reimbursed on the item (or use Select all).
  4. Save Your choices go into your HSA-Review folder.

! Doctor's note / LMN

A provider's letter is advisable for these. They are still reimbursable; the letter matters if records are questioned.

🩺 Medical receipts (from the year folder)

🏪 Store receipts

Confirmed: Amazon-marked items

Amazon marks these as FSA/HSA eligible. The item price is used.

Unconfirmed: not marked by Amazon

Amazon has not marked these as FSA/HSA eligible. Check each item before claiming. Rows that show only an order total use that total as an estimate.

Not counted: returned, cancelled, or never charged

These orders have no amount to claim. The reason comes from the order page.
